What Does a Low-Pass Filter Do?

Start with a 5 Hz square-wave signal contaminated by a 60 Hz disturbance. Change the cutoff frequency and watch what happens to both the noise and the desired signal.

Lower cutoff → stronger attenuation of higher-frequency components
First-order low-pass response
Each frequency component is scaled by G(f) = 1 / √(1 + (f/fc)²).
