Explore, Then Decide

  1. Start with 1 Hz @ 20 Hz. How many samples describe each cycle?
  2. Try 9 Hz @ 20 Hz. Nyquist is satisfied, but does the waveform look richly measured?
  3. Try 17 Hz @ 20 Hz. Why can the recorded data look like a 3 Hz oscillation?
  4. Increase fs until the 17 Hz motion is no longer aliased. What changed: the sensor bandwidth or the sampling process?
